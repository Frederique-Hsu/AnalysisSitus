Side milling

Side milled features correspond to the round faces represented with cylinders or generalized cylinders (surfaces of linear extrusion). Such faces are supposed to be finished by side of a mill.

Prismatic contour milling

A prismatic contour is a generalization of a pocket feature. This feature is composed of an optional bottom face and a series of walls perpendicular to that (possibly virtual) bottom. It is assumed that the wall faces are side milled. This feature also consumes fillets and rounds if they can be side milled.

Shoulder milling

A shoulder is a specific kind of contour milling feature. This feature consists of two mutually perpendicular sides and does not provide any fixed milling direction. Another distinctive property of a shoulder is its openness, which should put less constraint on the required tooling.

Fillet chains

Fillet chains that are not accommodated into other features are reported separately. Fillet chains that can be manufactured together with milled contours are reported as subfeatures of contours.
