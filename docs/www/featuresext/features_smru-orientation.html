SMRU automatic orientation

SMRU comes with predefined rules for automatic alignment of parts in 3D space. Normally, feature recognition operates in the part's as-designed position, and once the features are identified, their attributes are used to move the geometry in a more appropriate location. The relocation rules depend on the body type. The following paragraphs give some more information on these rules.

Flat shapes

The following rules are applied for alignment of flat sheet parts:

  • (1) Check if two sheet faces are parallel to the main projection planes.
  • (2) If (1), then find the longest straight edge: it should be aligned with one of the principal axes and contribute more than 40% of the bounding box's larger side.
  • (3) If (1) and (2), then the sheet face is said to be aligned properly. If not, the flat shape is realigned using the optimal orientation algorithm.

Folded sheet metals

The following rules are used for the alignment of folded sheet metals:

  • (1) Check if two sheet faces are parallel to the main projection planes.
  • (2) If not (1), the alignment transformation can be chosen by a couple of mutually orthogonal sheet faces, starting from the largest one.
  • (3) If not (2), the largest sheet face can be aligned to a projection plane, and its longest bend can be aligned to an axis.

Non-round tubes and profiles

The following rules are used for the alignment of non-round tubes and profiles:

  • (1) The recognized tube/profile axis is taken as the first "axis hint."
  • (2) Another axis is computed as a normal vector to the largest "sheet" face.
  • (3) With (1) and (2), the realignment transformation is computed.

Round tubes

The round tubes are realigned by their recognized axes.

Note on glTF export

If the -asm flag is passed to the SMRU executable asiSheetMetalExe, then the parts exported to glTF files do not get their calculated optimal transformation applied. This is done for the purpose of being able to recover the parts' original places in the scope of their assembly. I.e., if the optimal transformation matrices have happened to be applied, then recomposition of an assembly from the scene tree and the exported glTF parts would resulted in wrong locations of the assembly components.
